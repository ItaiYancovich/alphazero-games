Speed test

Times the bots on this device in two setups: before (one network worker, as the site used to run) and after (a pool of network workers, tuned to this device). They run after–before–before–after, so a phone that slows down as it warms up is fair to both.

Takes about 3–6 minutes. Keep this tab in front and the screen on; plugged in and not in low-power mode is best.

Seconds per move at 400 simulations (lower is better); for the v3 bot, which thinks for 2 seconds a move, simulations per second (higher is better).
